# Future Vision — 10-Year Roadmap

| Field | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Horizon | 10 years from founding blueprint baseline |
| Status | Directional — not a contractual delivery schedule |
| Companions | [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md) · [Product Vision](Product_Vision.md) |

## 1. Purpose

Describe the decade-scale AEOS ambition: Passport ubiquity, ecosystem marketplace, authority-ready evidence, advisory AI, and expansion into emerging aviation sectors — without claiming dates as commitments.

## 2. Horizons

| Years | Theme | Outcomes |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Y1–Y2 | Operational depth | MRO/Airline/CAMO hardening; Mobile; Passport packs; Connect webhooks |
| Y3–Y4 | Ecosystem | Developer Platform; Marketplace pilots; Academy; OEM packs; Twin services |
| Y5–Y6 | Network effects | Careers; verified suppliers; lessor network; AI advisors with citations |
| Y7–Y8 | Regulated edge | Authority portals; richer assurance; optional crypto anchoring |
| Y9–Y10 | New vehicles | UAV/eVTOL industry packs; Military classified deployments (separate ADRs) |

## 3. Product maturity targets (directional)

Core omnipresent; MRO/Airline/CAMO productized; Passport default for transfers; Marketplace liquid for apps/training; AI trusted as advisor; Twin used in MCC; Military/UAV only under explicit architecture.

## 4. Explicit non-claims

- Not a promise of authority product certification.
- Not autonomous aircraft release.
- Not replacement of OEM PLM or airline PSS.

## 5. Governance

Major horizon shifts require ADRs and roadmap updates. Honesty labels remain mandatory.
